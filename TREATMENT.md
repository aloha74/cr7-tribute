# TREATMENT — CR7 v3: "THE 900 CLUB"
Vox-style paper-cut sports documentary. 216s, 1920×1080, 30fps.
Music: "Anthem of Victory" (Pixabay, commercial use OK). 42 beat hits mapped.

## Logline
900+ goals — no human in football history has done it. A paper-cut documentary
asks HOW, and answers it in three acts: the boy, the proof, the meaning.

## Why this hooks non-fans (the v2 failure)
v2 was a slideshow: same rhythm every scene (kicker → headline → subline),
no question, no story, no reason to keep watching. v3 opens with a mystery
in the first 3 seconds, varies pace (montage ↔ breath), and earns every stat
through footage, not title cards.

## Three candidate structures (chosen: #2)
1. Chronological career — rejected: that's v2, a birthday card.
2. **Question-driven essay (Vox): one mystery → evidence → answer.** CHOSEN.
3. Countdown 900→0 — rejected: gimmicky, fights the music's build.

## Arc
- HOOK (0–16s): real goal footage at 0.7s + "900+ GOALS." / "No one in history." / "How?"
- ACT 1 — THE BOY (16–56s): paper map, Madeira → Lisbon. Intimate, slow.
- ACT 2 — THE STAGES (56–108s): paper journey map Manchester → Madrid → Turin.
  Stamps, photos in torn-paper frames. Pace picks up.
- ACT 3 — THE PROOF (108–152s): data story — goals-by-club bars drawn live,
  then REAL GOAL MONTAGE cut on beats. Fastest section.
- THE TURN (152–177s): music breathes. "Numbers don't explain THIS." SIUUU —
  the celebration the world copies. One held moment.
- ANSWER (177–201s): the opening question answered — obsession, not talent.
- ECHO (201–216s): bookend. "900+. And still counting." Credits.

## Shot grammar (paper-cut collage)
- Torn-paper frames hold every photo/footage (SVG jagged masks, drop shadows).
- Chapter transitions = paper actions: sheet peel / torn wipe. No fades.
- Data viz: bars drawn mark-by-mark, pinned handwritten-style notes.
- Journey map: paper shapes + dashed travel path drawn on hits.
- Kinetic type: questions slam on beats; answers rise slow.

## Sound
- Music grid first (42 hits). Goal montage: stadium ambience from footage
  ducked under music (J-cut in, L-cut out).
- Paper foley on transitions (tear/swish) — synthesized, subtle.
- One near-silence before THE TURN (152s): the breath before "THIS."

## Signature shot
THE TURN: footage of the SIUUU celebration inside a torn-paper circle that
expands to full frame on the beat — the paper "opens up" like the world
copying the celebration.
